About

I am a PhD student in Economics at the University of California, Irvine. I study international trade and trade policy, with a focus on how firms and regions respond to tariff shocks and policy uncertainty. My work combines customs records, plant-level registries and satellite data to measure these responses at a fine geographic scale.

Fields: International Trade, Trade Policy, Economic Geography

Work in Progress

Tariff Shocks, Policy Uncertainty, and the Response of Korean Manufacturing to the 2025–26 U.S. Tariff Regime
Abstract ▸

The 2025–26 U.S. tariffs on Korean vehicles, parts and other manufactures moved through an announcement, two implementation dates, a negotiated rate cut and a court ruling within a single year. Using monthly customs exports by product, destination and municipality, a geocoded registry of Korean plants, and satellite night-time lights, I separate the effects of announcements, uncertainty and tariff levels, measure how much of the tariff exporters absorbed, and trace spillovers to domestic suppliers.
