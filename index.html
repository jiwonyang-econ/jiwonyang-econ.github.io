About

I am a Ph.D. student in Economics at the University of California, Irvine. Before starting the Ph.D., I spent more than five years as a researcher at the Korea International Trade Association (KITA), analyzing trade flows, export outlooks and trade policy for Korean firms and policymakers.

My research interests lie in international trade and behavioral macroeconomics, particularly how uncertainty and expectations shape economic decisions, adaptation, and resilience.
